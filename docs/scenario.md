# Scenario: ShopNest

ShopNest is an online shopping start-up migrating its store from on-premises servers to the cloud.

**Business problem:** traffic grows steadily and spikes during festival sales, so the monthly bill is hard to predict
and wasteful resources go unnoticed.

**What this project does for ShopNest**
1. **Estimate** the monthly cost of the planned workload on AWS, Azure and GCP.
2. **Forecast** the next 30 days of spend from past billing, accounting for trend, weekdays and sale seasons.
3. **Detect anomalies** such as a forgotten instance causing a sudden cost jump.
4. **Recommend savings** (terminate idle VMs, schedule dev/test off-hours, right-size under-used servers).

All billing data is synthetic and generated by `python -m estimator.data_gen`.
